# CoopSense demo video plan

Target length: 2 to 3 minutes. Record it on a phone or laptop with a
screen recording, talking over the top as you click through the app.
The file `video/demo-walkthrough.webm` is a silent 33 second recording
of the same walkthrough, so you can see the order before you record.

## 0:00 - 0:20 | The problem

Show the home screen.

Say: "Hello, I am Ibrahim Yahaya from Bida, Niger State, Nigeria, a
student at the Federal University of Technology Minna. My family runs
a poultry farm, and I built CoopSense for small farmers like us. Feed
is our biggest cost, one sick bird can spread disease through the
whole flock, and a missed vaccine can wipe out weeks of work."

## 0:20 - 0:55 | Feed cost calculator

Stay on the Feed cost tab. Change the bird type to Broilers, then
back to Layers. Point at the results.

Say: "First, the feed calculator. I enter my flock size, the price of
a bag of feed and how much each bird eats. It tells me the feed I need
per day, what it costs per day and per month, and what each egg costs
me to produce. If the cost per egg is close to my selling price, I
know the margin is thin."

## 0:55 - 1:30 | Health check

Go to the Health check tab. Tick sudden deaths, twisted neck and
greenish diarrhoea. Click "Check my flock". Scroll down to the
results.

Say: "Next, the health check. I tick the signs I see in my flock, and
it lists the conditions that match best, with what to do right away.
It is a first guide, not a vet. If birds are dying, it tells me to
call a vet quickly."

## 1:30 - 2:00 | Egg tracker

Go to the Egg tracker tab. Show the chart and the averages. Add
today's egg count.

Say: "The egg tracker keeps my daily records. It shows my average
over the last seven days, my laying rate, and a forecast for the next
week based on my own records. If laying drops, I know to check the
flock."

## 2:00 - 2:30 | Vaccines and close

Go to the Vaccines tab. Enter a hatch date about 20 days ago. Point
at the due and overdue items and tick one off.

Say: "Last, the vaccine schedule. I enter the day my chicks hatched
and it works out when each vaccine is due. Everything runs in the
browser, no account and no server. Thank you."

## Recording tips

- Record in a quiet room and speak slowly.
- Keep the mouse still while you talk, then click.
- If you make a mistake, stop and start that part again. You can cut
  it out later.
- The video file for the submission should be 2 to 4 minutes long.
  Upload it to YouTube as unlisted and paste the link in the Devpost
  submission.
