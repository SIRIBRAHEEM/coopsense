## YouTube title

CoopSense - know what every egg costs you to produce

## YouTube description

Hello, I am Ibrahim Yahaya from Bida, Niger State, Nigeria, a student at the Federal University of Technology Minna. My family runs a poultry farm, and I built CoopSense for small farmers like us.

CoopSense is a free web app with four tools for small poultry farmers:

1. Feed cost calculator - enter flock size, feed price and daily intake, and see feed needed per day, cost per day and per month, and the feed cost per egg.
2. Health check - tick the signs you see in your flock and get the conditions that match best, with what to do right away. It is a first guide, not a vet diagnosis.
3. Egg tracker - log eggs each day and see the 7-day average, laying rate, a 14-day chart and a rough forecast for the next week.
4. Vaccination schedule - enter the hatch date and see when each vaccine is due, overdue or coming up.

Everything runs in the browser. No account, no server, no cost. Records are saved on your device, and it works offline after load.

Try the live demo: https://siribraheem.github.io/coopsense/
Source code: https://github.com/SIRIBRAHEEM/coopsense

Built by Ibrahim Yahaya, Bida, Niger State, Nigeria. Student at the Federal University of Technology Minna.
